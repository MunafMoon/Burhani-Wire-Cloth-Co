Perforated Wire Mesh

Perforated Wire Mesh is made with repeated holes or perforations for screening, filtration, protection and panel applications. It offers a strong sheet-like structure with controlled open area.

Perforated mesh can be selected by hole size, pitch, sheet thickness, material and panel dimensions. It is useful for machine guards, strainers, covers, ventilation panels and industrial screening.
