Copper Gasket

Copper Gaskets are sealing components used where thermal conductivity, compression and reliable sealing are important. They are commonly used in fittings, machine assemblies and industrial equipment.

Copper gaskets can be supplied in required inside diameter, outside diameter, thickness and quantity. They are useful in applications where the gasket must conform under pressure while maintaining sealing performance.
